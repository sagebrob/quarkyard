Cell Cycle Simulator

A digital version of the classroom Cell Game, for two players sharing one computer. Take turns rolling, move your cell around the board, and turn over the card at each p53 checkpoint to see whether your cell divides normally — or becomes a cancer cell.

Round 1: checkpoints are checked normally. The lighter Chance squares do nothing this round.
