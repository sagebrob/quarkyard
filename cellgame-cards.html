Skips ahead

"Causes cancer" cards flag the cell as cancerous the instant they're drawn, no matter where it is on the board — one skips the very next checkpoint without ever checking the scorecard, the other sends the cell straight to Finish. The three "destroyed" cards are p53 still doing its job even though it's mutated: the cell dies and restarts from scratch, resources reset to zero, same as failing a checkpoint outright — it does not count as a cancer cell. Everything else just changes position or costs a turn, and the cell's fate still gets decided honestly at whichever checkpoint it reaches next.
