Lab card options

Four ways to show each lab on the homepage. Each one uses the same six labs with rewritten one-line descriptions. Tell me a number, or mix pieces from more than one.

1Matched line icons

One simple drawing per lab, all in the same style: teal lines, a sage fill and one warm accent. It's the calmest and most uniform option, and it looks good at any size. Every new lab needs a new icon, which I can draw.

Heat Pump

Rising Seas

Life Science

Yeast & UV

Plate yeast, test sunscreen, cloth or foil in the sun, and count which colonies survive.

Life Science

Tumor Growth

Grow a benign and a malignant tumor side by side and compare what each does to the lung.
