One Cell's Journey

Play one cell through the cell cycle, one significant event at a time — every checkpoint, every Cellular Chance card that actually changes something, until it's destroyed, becomes cancerous, or divides successfully. No totals, no percentages — just this one cell's story. A destroyed cell's story ends there: it does not come back as a new cell in this same journey. (Cards like "extra roll" that don't change anything are resolved silently and skipped.)
