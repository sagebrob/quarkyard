Warm-Up Trait: Earlobe Attachment

Before we trace a real cancer-risk gene, let's practice basic Mendelian inheritance and Punnett squares with a simple, harmless trait: earlobe attachment.

F = free earlobes (dominant)
f = attached earlobes (recessive)
FF or Ff → free earlobes. Only ff → attached earlobes. Every person has two alleles: one from each parent.
